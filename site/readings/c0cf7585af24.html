Spatio-temporal physics-informed digital twins for early fault prognostics of wind turbine generators and gearboxes.

Tirupathi Trishitha, Jonna Nikhil, P V Manitha

大型风机传动链承受随机气动载荷，单纯数据模型与物理模型各有适用不足。本文提出时空物理信息数字孪生，将热力学和疲劳裂纹扩展规律融入深度学习，联合处理热异常、振动诊断和剩余寿命预测。[摘要]报告真实监测数据验证支持提前预警与低误报，并称优于常规基线。当前仅有摘要，缺少指标、实验配置和统计细节，适合作为物理约束智能运维的方法线索，而非已充分核验的性能结论。

研究问题

研究针对随机气动载荷下风机传动链的提前失效，重点涉及高速齿轮箱轴承与发电机定子绕组。[摘要]以“high false alarm rates”指出纯数据模型在暂态运行中的误报问题，并称物理模型难以捕捉场地环境导致的动态边界变化，形成混合建模的动机。

方法与路线

框架分三条技术路径：热异常采用稳态热力学基线与注意力时序卷积网络，以超阈值极值理论动态定阈；振动经希尔伯特解调后由多尺度卷积长短期记忆自编码器重构；寿命采用物理信息门控循环单元。[摘要]明确将“Paris-Erdogan crack propagation law”纳入损失函数。

创新与比较

解读：值得关注的是把不同物理规律分别嵌入热异常检测和疲劳寿命预测，而非仅将物理量作为输入。[摘要]强调“directly within deep learning architectures”，并提出在线发现疲劳参数；但摘要没有与既有物理信息数字孪生逐项比较，不能据此认定其首创性。

证据与发现

[摘要]报告真实SCADA及振动监测数据验证获得提前预警和低误报，并称“statistically outperforming”常规监督与无监督基线；还报告轴承疲劳参数收敛至理论疲劳磨损极限。摘要未给出提前量、误报率或统计检验结果，因此只能确认作者报告的效果方向。

局限与边界

当前资料的主要限制是证据粒度，而非已经证实的方法缺陷。[摘要]虽称使用“real SCADA datasets”，却未提供样本规模、故障类型、数据划分、基线配置和消融实验。跨机组泛化、在线计算负担及不确定性校准也无法从摘要判断，需阅读全文核查。

Utility-scale wind energy conversion systems are subjected to highly stochastic aerodynamic loading profiles, making drivetrain components-particularly high-speed gearbox shaft bearings and generator stator windings-susceptible to premature failure. Traditional predictive maintenance frameworks rely either on pure data-driven deep learning models, which suffer from high false alarm rates during transient operational profiles and frequently violate thermodynamic boundaries, or first-principles physical models, which fail to capture dynamic boundary variations due to site-specific environmental factors. This paper introduces a hybrid Spatio-Temporal Physics-Informed Digital Twin (PIDT) framework that integrates physical thermodynamic principles and material fatigue crack propagation laws directly within deep learning architectures. For SCADA thermal anomaly detection, the proposed approach combines a steady-state thermodynamic baseline model with a causal, dilated Physics-Informed Temporal Convolutional Network with Self-Attention (PI-TCN-Attn). Anomaly thresholds are dynamically derived using Peaks-Over-Threshold Extreme Value Theory fitting a Generalized Pareto Distribution to tail excesses. For vibration-based structural diagnostics, amplitude envelopes are extracted using Hilbert demodulation to isolate mesh defect energies, which are subsequently reconstructed via a Multi-Scale Convolutional LSTM Autoencoder. Remaining Useful Life is forecasted using a Physics-Informed Gated Recurrent Unit that incorporates monotonicity constraints and the Paris-Erdogan crack propagation law directly within the loss function, enabling the online discovery of material fatigue parameters. Experimental validation on real SCADA datasets and vibration condition monitoring data demonstrates that the hybrid twin provides early warning lead times for critical drivetrain failures while maintaining low false alarm rates, statistically outperforming conventional unsupervised and supervised machine learning baselines. Furthermore, the learned fatigue parameters of the bearings converge to theoretical fatigue wear limits, validating the physics-guided parameter discovery mechanism.
