Review on Structural Design and Performance Analysis of Wind Turbine Towers

Cheng Zhang
Archives of Current Research International

本文围绕风力机大型化、高塔化背景下的安全、耐久与经济性问题，综述钢管、混凝土、钢—混凝土混合及格构式塔架，重点讨论有限元分析在静力、疲劳、动力与抗震评估中的作用。摘要指出法兰连接、过渡区域及循环风荷载值得关注，并介绍机器学习预测、声发射监测与振动控制的结合趋势。本文适合作为塔架性能研究的入门索引，但当前仅有摘要，不能据此判断综述完整性或模型验证质量。[摘要]

研究问题

研究问题是机组容量与轮毂高度增加时，如何兼顾塔架结构安全、耐久性和成本效益。作者以“structural safety, durability, and cost-effectiveness”概括核心关切，并覆盖不同塔架形式；摘要未给出特定设计场景或量化目标。[摘要]

方法与路线

本文采用综述方式，整理四类塔架的结构设计与性能特征，以有限元分析为重点，原文称“particular emphasis on the role of finite element analysis”。分析涉及锥形壳体、连接、过渡段、材料及荷载；摘要未说明检索策略和纳入标准。[摘要]

创新与比较

解读：其潜在价值在于将多类塔架及静力、疲劳、动力问题置于共同的有限元分析框架下，而非提出已获验证的新算法。摘要提及“machine-learning-based prediction”等融合方向，但未证明这些方法由本文首创，也未提供足以判定原创性的比较证据。[摘要]

证据与发现

综述归纳认为，静力评估应关注“flange connections and transition regions”，疲劳则受循环风荷载与塔架特定荷载条件显著影响。动力与抗震分析还需考虑固有频率控制、长周期地震动、近场效应及土—结构相互作用；摘要未报告定量比较结果。[摘要]

局限与边界

当前证据仅限摘要，缺少文献筛选流程、研究覆盖范围、模型验证资料及不确定性处理信息，因此无法核实综述的系统性与结论稳健性。“high-fidelity representation”是摘要对有限元能力的表述，并不能替代具体误差验证；这些是资料限制，不是已确认的论文缺陷。[摘要]

方向关联

解读：与结构设计的联系最直接，包括应力、变形与屈曲评估；与可靠性研究的联系来自“fatigue-damage accumulation”，但尚无概率可靠性证据。机器学习及监测提供智能运维线索；摘要未涉及生成式设计机制，不能将一般设计优化等同于生成式设计。[摘要]

后续研究建议

建议：后续获取全文时，优先核查连接与过渡段的建模假设、疲劳荷载谱及验证依据，并追踪监测与数值模型融合的具体案例。可探索用监测信息更新有限元模型并评估寿命预测不确定性；该设想受“digital-twin”展望启发，不是本文已完成的成果。[摘要]

Wind turbine towers are critical supporting structures whose structural safety, durability, and cost-effectiveness become increasingly important as turbine capacities and hub heights increase. This review synthesises the structural design and performance characteristics of steel tubular, concrete, steel–concrete hybrid, and lattice wind turbine towers, with particular emphasis on the role of finite element analysis (FEA). The review considers mechanical behaviour, durability, static response, fatigue performance, and dynamic and seismic characteristics. FEA enables high-fidelity representation of tapered shells, connections, transition sections, material behaviour, and relevant loading conditions, thereby supporting evaluation of stress distributions, deformation, local stress concentrations, buckling resistance, and fatigue-damage accumulation. The reviewed studies indicate that flange connections and transition regions require particular attention in static assessment, while fatigue performance is strongly influenced by cyclic wind loading and tower-specific loading conditions. Modal and seismic analyses further show the importance of natural-frequency control, long-period ground motions, near-field effects, and soil–structure interaction in the dynamic response of wind turbine towers. Emerging approaches combine FEA with machine-learning-based prediction, acoustic-emission monitoring, and vibration-control devices. Overall, FEA provides an effective framework for analysing the principal structural performance aspects of wind turbine towers and for supporting design optimisation, durability assessment, and life-cycle performance evaluation. Future applications may increasingly integrate numerical modelling with digital-twin and structural-health-monitoring approaches.
