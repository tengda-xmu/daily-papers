A topology-constrained multimodal LLM agent for fault diagnosis in highway electromechanical systems

高速公路机电系统的服务中断可能来自供电、网络、设备协作或配置变化，单一故障表象难以直接对应根因。本文提出拓扑约束的多模态大语言模型智能体，结合物理依赖与业务协作两类拓扑，将资产、告警、工单和电气信号等组织为诊断证据包，支持根因排序、解释及维修建议。摘要报告其优于多类基线，但未提供具体指标、提升幅度或实验细节，适合作为智能运维方法线索阅读。[摘要]

研究问题

研究面向高速公路异构机电设备的故障诊断：可见服务中断可能由供电退化、网络不稳、单设备故障、设备协作错误或系统配置变化引起。摘要以“visible service interruption”说明诊断入口。解读：关键问题是从业务表象追溯跨设备根因，而非仅识别孤立设备状态。[摘要]

方法与路线

框架融合资产记录、设备状态、告警、工单、车道业务事件、环境测量及瞬态电气信号，建立物理基础设施拓扑与业务协作拓扑。二者用于构建“structured diagnostic evidence package”，再交由大语言模型开展根因排序、证据解释、维修推荐和工单起草。[摘要]

创新与比较

摘要明确的方法特点是将物理依赖和业务协作两类拓扑共同引入诊断，并以多模态证据包连接拓扑信息与语言模型。解读：其潜在贡献在于让推理依托显式关系和证据，而不只是文本检索生成；现有资料不足以证明这一组合的首创性，也未披露约束机制的实现细节。[摘要]

查看公开摘要

Highway electromechanical systems contain heterogeneous equipment such as lane controllers, license plate recognition cameras, barriers, switches, UPS units, power distribution cabinets, servers, sensors, and operation platforms. In daily maintenance, a visible service interruption may originate from power supply degradation, network instability, single-device failure, inter-device collaboration errors, or system-level configuration changes. This paper proposes a topology-constrained multimodal large language model (LLM) agent framework for evidence-based fault diagnosis and human-in-the-loop maintenance decision support. The framework integrates asset records, device states, alarms, work orders, lane business events, environmental measurements, and transient electrical signals. Two complementary topologies are constructed: a physical infrastructure topology for power, network, cabinet, and device dependencies, and a business collaboration topology for lane-side event chains and platform services. Based on the dual topology, the system builds a structured diagnostic evidence package that is used by an LLM agent for root-cause ranking, evidence explanation, maintenance recommendation, and work-order drafting. Experiments are conducted on an anonymized highway electromechanical maintenance dataset. Compared with rule-based diagnosis, text-only retrieval-augmented generation, structured classifier, and graph-based classifiers, the proposed method improves fault classification, root-cause localization, and recommendation acceptance rate. The results indicate that topology constraints and multimodal evidence packaging can constrain diagnostic reasoning with explicit evidence and make LLM agents more reliable for highway electromechanical maintenance.
