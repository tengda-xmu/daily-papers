A Heterogeneous Multi-Modal Parallel Deep Learning Framework for Multiaxial Fatigue Life Prediction of Metals

Jibin Li, Jun Han, Jun Wang, Yudi Ai, Qi Lei
Technologies

本文针对复杂非比例载荷下金属多轴疲劳寿命预测，提出融合材料属性、载荷历史与局部应变模式的三分支网络，输出lg(Nf)。摘要报告，模型在包含40种金属、1167个样本的公开数据集固定划分上取得R2为0.9052；消融支持分支互补及注意力机制作用，但五折交叉验证提示性能增益仍需更大数据集验证。[摘要] 本次仅作摘要级解读，适合关注疲劳可靠性与数据驱动寿命评估的读者。

研究问题

研究问题是复杂非比例加载下的多轴疲劳寿命预测。摘要指出传统临界平面与能量模型依赖经验假设，难以刻画路径相关损伤演化，原文称“path-dependent damage evolution”。[摘要] 解读：关键挑战不仅是拟合寿命，还包括表达不同加载路径带来的损伤差异。

方法与路线

方法采用“three-branch parallel neural architecture”：DNN提取材料固有属性，带多头自注意力的BiGRU处理载荷历史长期依赖，1D-CNN提取局部应变波动；三路融合后输出lg(Nf)。[摘要] 摘要未给出网络规模、损失函数和训练配置，尚不能据此复现。

创新与比较

摘要所述方法特色是将材料静态信息、载荷时间依赖和局部应变模式交由不同网络处理，再融合为损伤表征，原文称“comprehensive damage representation”。[摘要] 解读：值得关注的是异构信息的互补组织方式；缺少全文及相关工作对比，无法确认其首创性。

证据与发现

摘要报告公开数据集包含40种金属、1167个样本，固定划分取得R2为0.9052，并称消融显示三分支互补，融合与注意力机制对性能增益重要。[摘要] 其证据同时包含限定语“on a fixed data split”；未提供各消融数值，不能量化单个模块的贡献。

局限与边界

摘要明确指出五折交叉验证提示性能增益“requires further validation with larger datasets”。[摘要] 因此现有结果不能直接证明跨数据划分的稳定优势。当前资料未给出各折指标、划分规则和外推测试，无法判断波动幅度或是否存在数据泄漏；这属于证据缺口，而非已证实缺陷。

方向关联

解读：模型直接预测金属多轴疲劳寿命，与结构设计中的寿命评估和疲劳可靠性方向紧密相关；摘要定位为“fatigue life assessment”。[摘要] 对智能运维，其载荷历史建模可提供方法参考，但资料未报告在线监测、剩余寿命更新或失效概率估计，不能将这些视为已有成果。

查看公开摘要

Multiaxial fatigue damage remains a critical barrier to the reliable design of metallic engineering components, particularly under complex non-proportional loading paths. Conventional critical-plane and energy-based models rely heavily on empirical assumptions and struggle to capture intricate path-dependent damage evolution, leading to limited accuracy and generalizability. To overcome these challenges, we propose a novel data-driven framework that integrates heterogeneous information sources through a three-branch parallel neural architecture. Specifically, a deep neural network (DNN) extracts static material intrinsic properties; a bidirectional gated recurrent unit (BiGRU) enhanced with a multi-head self-attention mechanism captures long-term temporal dependencies and adaptively weights key stages in loading histories; and a one-dimensional convolutional neural network (1D-CNN) extracts local strain fluctuation patterns. The three streams are fused to produce a comprehensive damage representation for end-to-end life prediction. The output of the model is the base −10 logarithm of fatigue life, i.e., lg(Nf). Evaluated on a public multiaxial fatigue dataset comprising 40 metals and 1167 samples, the proposed model achieves a coefficient of determination (R2) of 0.9052, achieving promising performance on a fixed data split (R2 = 0.9052); ablation studies demonstrate the complementary nature of the three feature extraction branches, though 5-fold cross-validation suggests that the performance gain requires further validation with larger datasets. Systematic ablation studies confirm that the multi-source fusion strategy and the attention mechanism are pivotal to the performance gain. This work offers a robust and effective data-driven solution for high-accuracy fatigue life assessment, and points toward future integration of physical knowledge to further enhance extrapolation and transparency.
