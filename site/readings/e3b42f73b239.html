公开摘要

Urban renewal requires intelligent service facility reconfiguration to balance accessibility, cost and stakeholder preferences. Traditional optimization models lack adaptability to dynamic contexts and qualitative inputs. This paper proposes a novel framework integrating Large Language Models (LLMs) with multi-agent collaboration for the facility location problem, formulating it as a p-median model enhanced by LLM-derived dynamic demand weighting and multi-agent reinforcement learning. Experiments on a synthetic urban dataset show 94.2% coverage efficiency and 23.4% lower negotiation overhead, indicating LLM-enhanced multi-agent systems are promising for intelligent urban infrastructure planning.
