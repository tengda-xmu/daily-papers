Quantum-Enhanced and Machine Learning Framework for High-Accuracy Fatigue Life Prediction and Structural Analysis of Connecting Rod

P. Venkatesh, P. S. Rani

本文面向110 cc四冲程汽油机连杆选材，将有限元分析、深度神经网络代理、多准则模糊排序及量子启发优化串联，并用经典疲劳关系评估寿命。[摘要]网络基于1,500个仿真样本训练，应力预测达到“R2 = 0.987”；作者据疲劳安全系数推荐42CrMo4合金钢。解读：其价值主要在结构选材与疲劳评估流程整合，不能由应力拟合精度直接推出寿命预测准确。当前仅有摘要，量子优化增益与实物验证仍缺少证据。

研究问题

研究针对110 cc四冲程汽油机连杆的材料选择，背景是提升功率重量比的需求。摘要以“most suitable material”为目标，并考察拉伸、压缩载荷下的结构响应。[摘要]解读：核心是材料性能与成本的多准则权衡，而非已展示的连杆几何生成设计。

方法与路线

作者用Pro/ENGINEER建模、ANSYS计算应力应变及位移，以DNN充当“fast surrogate model”，结合Takagi–Sugeno模糊推理排序和模拟器上的QAOA搜索。[摘要]疲劳计算采用Basquin关系、Goodman修正与Miner累积损伤规则，摘要未交代各模块接口。

创新与比较

摘要将框架描述为“multilayer optimization framework”，整合代理预测、模糊排序及量子启发搜索。[摘要]解读：可辨识的特点是方法组合，现有资料不能确认各模块具有原创算法贡献。QAOA运行于状态向量模拟器，不等于量子硬件验证，也未证明量子加速。

证据与发现

应力预测报告“RMSE = 2.41 MPa”、MAPE为0.71%，并称优于随机森林和XGBoost。[摘要]AA-7075与42CrMo4的疲劳安全系数分别为0.78和1.97，作者推荐后者。解读：“无限寿命”应限于所用模型及载荷条件，不应理解为实际服役的无条件保证。

局限与边界

摘要虽声称“improved predictive accuracy”，但未提供数据划分、独立试验验证、载荷谱、材料疲劳参数来源或优化消融结果。[摘要]解读：这些属于摘要层面的证据缺口，不能断言全文未做；应力代理拟合指标也不足以单独验证疲劳寿命预测误差。

方向关联

与结构设计和疲劳可靠性的联系直接，摘要明确采用“Miner's linear damage accumulation rule”评估累积损伤。[摘要]解读：代理模型可为快速方案筛选提供思路，但材料搜索不等于生成式设计；对智能运维的联系仅是潜在迁移，摘要未涉及在线监测或维护决策。

查看公开摘要

The connecting rod is a critical mechanical component that transmits reciprocating motion from the piston to rotational motion in the crankshaft. Although connecting rods are traditionally manufactured from aluminum alloys, titanium alloys, or alloy steels, the demand for higher power-to-weight ratios has motivated the exploration of alternative materials supported by advanced computational validation methods. This study proposes a multilayer optimization framework for identifying the most suitable material for the connecting rod of a 110 cc four-stroke petrol engine. The component was modelled in Pro/ENGINEER, and high-fidelity Finite Element Analysis (FEA) was performed in ANSYS to evaluate the von Mises stress, total strain, and displacement under tensile and compressive loading. A deep neural network (DNN), trained on 1,500 FEA-generated samples, was employed as a fast surrogate model, whereas a Takagi–Sugeno fuzzy inference system was used for the multi-criteria material ranking. A quantum-inspired optimization layer, implemented as a Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) executed on the IBM Qiskit Aer state-vector simulator, was used to search the multidimensional design space of alloy candidates and cost-to-performance ratios. The fatigue life was quantified using the Basquin stress–life relation with Goodman's mean stress correction and Miner's linear damage accumulation rule. The DNN surrogate reproduced the FEA results with R2 = 0.987, RMSE = 2.41 MPa, and MAPE = 0.71% for stress prediction, outperforming the Random Forest and XGBoost baselines. Fatigue assessment revealed that AA-7075 operates above its endurance limit (fatigue safety factor 0.78), whereas 42CrMo4 alloy steel provides an infinite-life performance (fatigue safety factor 1.97) and is therefore recommended. The integration of quantum-inspired optimization and deep neural networks has demonstrated improved predictive accuracy compared with that of conventional simulation approaches.
