The integration of the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and advanced machine learning represents a paradigm shift in the life-cycle management, reliability, and energy sustainability of intelligent mechanical systems. In modern rotating machinery, electrified automotive drivetrains, and precision manufacturing equipment, mechanical degradation phenomena—such as rolling contact fatigue, subsurface spalling, gear tooth pitting, and lubricant shear breakdown—inexorably degrade operational efficiency, induce catastrophic downtime, and drive up parasitic energy dissipation. Traditional maintenance paradigms rely heavily on either reactive breakdown repairs or rigid, calendar-based preventive overhauls. The former risks catastrophic structural failure and massive secondary damage, while the latter incurs severe economic penalties through the premature disposal of components retaining substantial residual life.
