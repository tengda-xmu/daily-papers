公开摘要

AI-Driven Model Development is a major paradigm shift in modern systems engineering, software-intensive engineering, manufacturing, digital-twin development, and Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) where AI is increasingly playing a key role in model creation and evolution by accelerating, automating, analyzing and improving the engineering model. Typical model development is often based on the practice of building models manually based on domain knowledge, requirements, design assumptions, system relationships, and operational information by experienced engineers. Manual modeling can be time-consuming, hard to maintain, and can be prone to inconsistencies as systems become increasingly complex, multidisciplinary, software-intensive, interconnected, and adaptive. AI approaches aim to complement this by leveraging AI tools such as machine learning, NLP, generative AI, knowledge graphs, computer vision, optimization algorithms, and intelligent agents to support engineers throughout the modeling lifecycle.
