本书围绕物联网、区块链与人工智能及机器学习的融合，介绍从计算基础、感知通信到可信机制和智能处理的技术体系，并延伸至数字孪生、联邦学习等主题。摘要称其连接“foundational theory”与系统设计及部署，应用覆盖智能电网、交通、农业和医疗。[摘要] 解读：适合作为智能运维相关技术的背景读物，但当前材料未呈现具体运维算法、实验结果或性能比较，不宜据此认定其具有突出的原创研究贡献。

研究问题

摘要关注多种计算技术如何共同支撑现代数字基础设施，强调其价值在于“their synergy”，而非单项技术的孤立发展。[摘要] 解读：核心问题是感知、信任与智能处理如何形成协同体系；材料未提出明确的研究假设、运维故障类型或可量化优化目标。

方法与路线

本书按基础计算、智能互联系统、区块链、智能算法和融合架构组织内容，涉及传感网络、共识机制、深度网络及联邦学习。[摘要] 解读：这里的方法主要体现为教材式知识组织和系统设计介绍，不能等同于作者提出并验证了一套新算法或实验流程。

创新与比较

摘要将数字孪生、零知识机器学习、智能体人工智能等列为融合架构中的前沿主题，并使用“unified architectures”描述综合方向。[摘要] 解读：跨技术整合是其内容特色，但主题新颖不等于原创贡献；当前没有证据确认作者提出了新的模型、协议或理论。

证据与发现

摘要描述物联网承担现实数据采集、区块链提供安全与去中心化信任、人工智能及机器学习支持自主决策，并列出多个应用领域。[摘要] 这些是书中技术定位和覆盖范围，不是实验发现；材料没有报告准确率、延迟、能耗或可靠性改善等可核验结果。

局限与边界

当前只有完整摘要，没有章节原文、案例过程或实验数据，因而无法核对技术实现深度、案例有效性和适用条件。[摘要] 解读：这是本次判断的证据局限，不是已证实的作品缺陷；也不能仅因涵盖主题较多，就断言内容浅显或融合方案不可行。

方向关联

摘要提到“digital twins”以及安全、韧性的信息物理系统，与智能运维的感知接入和系统建模存在主题联系。[摘要] 解读：这些内容可能帮助梳理运维架构，但摘要没有直接讨论故障诊断、寿命预测、结构优化或可靠性量化，不能宣称已解决这些任务。

后续研究建议

阅读建议

The rapid convergence of the Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Machine Learning (ML) is fundamentally reshaping the landscape of modern digital infrastructure. While each of these paradigms has individually driven massive technological leaps, their true transformative power lies in their synergy. IoT acts as the sensory network collecting real-world data, Blockchain provides an immutable layer of security and decentralized trust, and AI/ML serves as the cognitive brain processing this information to enable real-time, autonomous decision-making. Advanced Computing Technologies: IoT, Blockchain, AI, and Machine Learning offers a comprehensive exploration of these core pillars, bridging foundational theory with real-world system design, architectural frameworks, and deployment strategies.The text begins by establishing core computing paradigms, historical evolution, hardware architectures, distributed systems, and the socio-economic drivers of Industry 4.0. It then transitions into the mechanics of intelligent connected systems, detailing sensor networks, embedded devices, communication protocols from MQTT to 5G and 6G, and the evolution toward the Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT). From there, the book examines cryptographic primitives, consensus mechanisms, smart contract development, and decentralized security models within Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technologies. Turning to the intelligence layer, it thoroughly explores classical machine learning, deep neural networks, transformer architectures, computer vision, Generative AI, Explainable AI, and privacy-preserving Federated Learning. Finally, the narrative synthesizes these pillars into unified architectures, showcasing cutting-edge developments such as digital twins, zero-knowledge machine learning (zkML), agentic AI, quantum paradigms, and sustainable computing.Tailored for undergraduate and graduate students, academic researchers, solution architects, and technology leaders, this book balances rigorous conceptual foundations with practical case studies spanning smart grids, autonomous transport, precision agriculture, and digital healthcare. By examining both individual components and integrated ecosystems, this work provides a complete roadmap for designing scalable, secure, and resilient cyber-physical systems. Whether utilized as a university textbook or an industry reference guide, this volume aims to empower readers to navigate, innovate, and lead in an increasingly interconnected and intelligent digital world.
