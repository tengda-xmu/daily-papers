本文是一篇面向天线设计优化的综述，关注设计变量增多时，反复全波电磁仿真带来的计算负担。摘要梳理传统方法、元启发式优化、机器学习及混合方法，并重点讨论代理建模与主动学习如何减少昂贵仿真。其阅读价值在于比较不同路线的数据依赖、仿真需求与计算效率，而非提供已验证的新算法。当前仅有摘要，无法核查具体案例、性能增益和综述覆盖质量。[摘要]

研究问题

研究问题是如何降低复杂天线设计对重复全波电磁仿真的依赖。摘要指出，传统解析关系、经验公式和参数扫描在设计变量增加时计算代价上升；紧凑、宽带、高性能及应用专用需求构成设计背景，但摘要没有给出具体优化指标或约束实例。[摘要]

方法与路线

本文采用综述方式，梳理传统设计、遗传算法与粒子群优化，再延伸到机器学习、深度学习、混合优化和主动学习。摘要称比较维度包括设计策略、优化技术、仿真需求、数据依赖及计算效率；未交代文献检索范围、纳入标准和质量评价程序。[摘要]

创新与比较

摘要呈现的组织重点是把代理建模与自适应样本选择共同置于减少昂贵仿真的框架中。解读：其潜在贡献在于关联预测、搜索和采样三类环节，而不是提出新的天线结构或优化算法；仅凭摘要不能确认这一综述视角相对已有综述具有原创性。[摘要]

The rapid development of wireless communication systems has created an increasing demand for compact, broadband, high-performance, and application-specific antennas. Conventional antenna design approaches generally rely on analytical formulations, empirical relationships, parameter sweeps, and repeated full-wave electromagnetic (EM) simulations, which become computationally expensive as the number of design variables increases. To address these challenges, metaheuristic optimisation techniques such as Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Particle Swarm Optimisation (PSO) have been widely investigated for exploring complex antenna design spaces. More recently, machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) approaches have been introduced for antenna performance prediction, inverse design, and surrogate-assisted optimisation. These approaches can reduce the computational burden associated with repeated EM simulations; however, their effectiveness often depends on sufficiently large and representative simulation datasets. Hybrid ML–metaheuristic approaches further improve the optimisation process by combining predictive models with intelligent search algorithms, while Active Learning enables the selective acquisition of informative simulation samples. This review presents the evolution of computational antenna design from conventional and metaheuristic approaches to ML-based, hybrid, and Active Learning-driven methodologies. The reviewed studies are critically examined with respect to their design strategies, optimisation techniques, simulation requirements, data dependence, and computational efficiency. Particular emphasis is placed on the role of surrogate modelling and adaptive sample selection in reducing expensive full-wave simulations. The review identifies the limitations of existing approaches and highlights the need for autonomous, data-efficient, and simulation-efficient frameworks for high-dimensional microstrip antenna optimisation.
