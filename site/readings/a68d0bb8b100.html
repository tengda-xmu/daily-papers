Metamaterials as transducers for structural health monitoring: a critical review and perspective

本文综述超材料用于结构健康监测的材料中心化路径：通过设计材料架构，将结构响应转换为可测信息，缓解传统传感系统的复杂性、布线和能耗等负担。作者按视觉、电磁和波动三类信息组织进展，并讨论逆向设计、拓扑优化、增材制造、机器学习与数字孪生等支撑技术。解读：其阅读价值主要在于梳理材料架构与感知功能的联系，而非提供已获验证的通用监测方案；当前依据仅为摘要。[摘要]

研究问题

传统结构健康监测依赖分布式传感器、数据采集、信号传输和外部处理。摘要指出其受到“system complexity, wiring, power consumption”等因素限制。核心问题是：材料能否直接参与感知，减少监测对附加系统的依赖？[摘要]

方法与路线

本文是综述，按生成信息的物理域组织已有进展。视觉域包括形变、结构色等，电磁域包括电容、压电及无源无线响应，波动域包括带隙、共振偏移和局域化等。解读：这一分类便于比较不同信息载体，但摘要未交代文献检索和筛选方法。[摘要]

创新与比较

摘要以“material-centric SHM”概括材料中心化监测方向，强调架构材料不仅承载，还可编码、转换和传递结构信息。解读：其概念价值在于将材料构型与信息处理联系起来；仅凭摘要，不能认定作者提出了新的器件、算法或实验机制。[摘要]

证据与发现

摘要归纳的主要进展是，超材料架构可将结构响应转为多种可测信息，并将感知、滤波、放大及通信作为多功能设计目标。其中“mechanical filtering”体现了机械响应参与信息处理的路径。但摘要没有具体试验、量化性能或对照结果。[摘要]

局限与边界

当前只提供摘要，无法核查各类方案的检测精度、适用载荷、长期稳定性及规模化效果。摘要仅称将讨论“future opportunities and challenges”，没有列出具体内容。因此这些问题属于资料尚未覆盖的核查事项，不能写成作者已证实的技术局限。[摘要]

方向关联

摘要明确列出“inverse design, topology optimization”等支撑技术，与结构设计方向直接相关；监测主题也切合智能运维。解读：可关注几何架构如何影响可观测响应，以及这些响应如何服务状态识别，但摘要不足以确认其已实现寿命预测或可靠性评估。[摘要]

查看公开摘要

Structural health monitoring (SHM) has traditionally relied on distributed sensors, data acquisition systems, signal transmission, and external data processing to assess the condition of engineering structures. Although this sensor-centric paradigm enables reliable damage detection, it remains limited by system complexity, wiring, power consumption, maintenance, and scalability. Recent advances in metamaterials offer an alternative approach in which the material itself participates in sensing by transducing structural responses into measurable information through its engineered architecture. This review examines how architected metamaterials encode, transform, and communicate structural information for next-generation SHM. Recent developments are organized according to the physical domain of the generated information: visual information, including shape transformation, structural colour, mechanochromism, and mechanoluminescence; electromagnetic information, including electrical, capacitive, piezoelectric, triboelectric, radio-frequency, metasurface, and passive wireless responses; and wave information, including elastic-wave propagation, bandgaps, resonance shifts, mode conversion, localization, and mechanical filtering;. Enabling technologies, including inverse design, topology optimization, additive manufacturing, machine learning, and digital twins, are highlighted for developing multifunctional metamaterials capable of sensing, filtering, amplifying, and communicating structural information. Finally, future opportunities and challenges are outlined for material-centric SHM, in which architected materials evolve from passive structural components into intrinsically intelligent systems that physically process and communicate information before it reaches conventional electronic systems.
