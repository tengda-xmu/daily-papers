Multiscale Leakage Modeling of PEMFC Gasket–MEA Frame Interfaces: A Mini-Review

Jin-Cun Han, Y. Xing, Ju-Yong Cao, Xiao-Bing Zhang, Ying Wang, Fu-Yong Yang

本文是一篇面向重载质子交换膜燃料电池密封可靠性的简评，聚焦垫片—膜电极组件边框的柔性—柔性接触界面。摘要梳理表面重构、材料老化与接触演化、界面泄漏和材料体相渗透的竞争，以及多尺度输运和不确定性预测方法。核心认识是密封评价不能停留于初始装配状态，而应关联服役中的接触与气体损失演化。作者提出原位验证、基准数据集和物理信息机器学习等未来需求；本解读仅依据摘要，不涉及全文实验核验。[摘要]

研究问题

研究问题是如何将密封可靠性评价从初始装配拓展到长期服役。摘要强调“beyond the initial assembly state”，对象为垫片—膜电极组件边框的柔性—柔性接触界面。其关注点是界面接触与气体损失如何共同演化，以回应重载应用的耐久性要求。[摘要]

方法与路线

本文采用综述梳理而非报告单一新算法。摘要列举“PSD)- and fractal-based surface reconstruction methods”，并涉及黏弹—超弹性、化学—热—力学描述，以及滑移流和克努森尺度输运。流固耦合、随机公差分析和代理辅助预测也属于其讨论范围。[摘要]

创新与比较

解读：摘要所呈现的价值主要是跨尺度和跨机制的组织，而非可确认的原创模型。作者将几何表征、材料老化、接触压力保持及气体输运纳入同一综述视野，并讨论从确定性模拟转向“uncertainty-aware and data-assisted frameworks”；是否具有首创性尚无证据。[摘要]

证据与发现

摘要明确指出气体损失涉及随服役条件变化的机制转换，即“pressure-driven interfacial leakage”与“temperature-sensitive bulk permeation”之间的转换。解读：不能始终以单一泄漏机制解释密封表现；摘要未给出转换阈值、主导区间或定量贡献。[摘要]

局限与边界

证据边界是仅有摘要，无法核查文献筛选流程、模型适用条件及实验对照。作者将“in situ experimental validation”和基准数据集列为未来需求，但这不等于文中完全没有验证。摘要也未提供误差、样本规模或寿命预测效果，不能据此评价模型成熟度。[摘要]

方向关联

与智能运维的联系直接来自“health-state assessment and predictive maintenance”，即作者展望的健康状态评估与预测性维护。解读：随机公差和接触压力保持分析也可支持结构可靠性研究；与生成式设计的联系则是潜在延伸，摘要没有提出生成模型或自动设计流程。[摘要]

后续研究建议

建议：优先围绕界面泄漏与体相渗透的区分开展验证，设计覆盖装配载荷、温度和老化状态的观测方案，再评估代理模型能否表达预测不确定性。该建议呼应作者的“lifecycle-oriented interface modeling”需求，但具体变量选择、实验方案和评价标准均需另行论证。[摘要]

查看公开摘要

To meet the stringent durability requirements of proton exchange membrane fuel cells (PEMFCs) for heavy-duty applications, sealing reliability must be evaluated beyond the initial assembly state and linked to the long-term evolution of interfacial contact and gas-loss behavior. This mini-review focuses on gasket–MEA frame sealing interfaces in PEMFCs, with particular emphasis on flexible–flexible contact. It systematically examines recent progress in multiscale geometric characterization and reconstruction, constitutive and contact evolution under assembly and service loads, the competition between interfacial leakage and bulk material permeation, and the development of predictive modeling strategies from deterministic simulations to uncertainty-aware and data-assisted frameworks. The review highlights power spectral density (PSD)- and fractal-based surface reconstruction methods, discusses visco-hyperelastic and chemo-thermo-mechanical descriptions relevant to elastomer aging and contact-pressure retention, and clarifies that gas-loss behavior depends on a service-dependent transition between pressure-driven interfacial leakage and temperature-sensitive bulk permeation. It further summarizes multiscale transport modeling approaches involving slip-flow and Knudsen-scale effects, as well as recent advances in fluid–structure interaction, stochastic tolerance analysis, and surrogate-assisted prediction. Finally, the review outlines key future needs in lifecycle-oriented interface modeling, in situ experimental validation, benchmark data sets, and the integration of physics-informed machine learning with multiscale leakage prediction for health-state assessment and predictive maintenance of PEMFC stacks.
