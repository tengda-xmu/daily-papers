Machine Learning for Solar Thermal Energy Optimization and Smart Control

本章综述人工智能与机器学习在太阳能热利用系统设计、运行、诊断和控制中的应用，涵盖平板集热器、真空管集热器及聚光太阳能电站。内容围绕性能代理建模、辐照与热输出预测、故障检测和预测性维护、智能控制四类任务展开。摘要报告相关控制策略可使年度有效供热量提高8%至15%，但未交代具体实验条件。本文适合作为智能运维方向的领域入口，不能据此确认各类算法的实际优劣。[摘要]

研究问题

本章关注机器学习如何服务太阳能热利用系统的设计、运行、诊断与控制，研究对象覆盖三类集热或发电设施。摘要以“design, operation, diagnosis, and control”界定范围，说明讨论不限于单一预测任务；但未给出各类系统的具体运行约束和问题优先级。[摘要]

本章采用综述方式梳理人工神经网络、支持向量回归、梯度提升、长短期记忆网络、Transformer、物理信息神经网络及深度强化学习，并按四类应用组织内容。摘要使用“methodologies surveyed”，未披露文献检索、筛选及质量评价程序，不能认定其符合系统综述规范。[摘要]

查看公开摘要

This chapter presents a systematic examination of how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques are transforming the design, operation, diagnosis, and control of solar thermal energy systems, including flat-plate collectors, evacuated tube collectors, and concentrating solar power plants. Principal ML methodologies surveyed include Artificial Neural Networks, Support Vector Regression, Gradient Boosting, Long Short-Term Memory networks, Transformer architectures, Physics-Informed Neural Networks, and Deep Reinforcement Learning. Applications are reviewed across four domains data-driven surrogate models for thermal performance prediction, ML-based irradiance and thermal output forecasting, intelligent fault detection and predictive maintenance frameworks, and AI-driven control strategies shown to improve annual useful heat delivery by 8 to 15 percent. Research challenges including data scarcity, model interpretability, and cross-site generalization are identified, with future directions proposed in federated learning, physics-informed architectures, and explainable AI.
