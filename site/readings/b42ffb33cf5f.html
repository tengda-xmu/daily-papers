The AI-FinTech Entrepreneurial Ecosystem: Exploring How Intelligent Financial Technologies and Algorithmic Digital Visibility Transform Opportunity Recognition and Venture Growth. A Comparative Study of Pakistan and China

Muhammad Ali Qazi, Haris Mehmood, Rashid Pervez, Abdul Mannan, Muhammad Irfan Syed
Journal of Business Insight and Innovation

公开摘要

Artificial intelligence and financial technology are increasingly intertwined with the digital platforms through which entrepreneurs discover opportunities, secure finance, reach customers, and scale ventures. This qualitative comparative study develops an integrated view of the AI-FinTech entrepreneurial ecosystem by examining how intelligent financial technologies and algorithmic digital visibility shape opportunity recognition and venture growth in Pakistan and China. Semi-structured interviews were conducted with 20 entrepreneurs, equally divided between the two countries, and the data were analyzed using reflexive thematic analysis. The analysis identifies six interrelated themes: AI-FinTech as an opportunity-sensing infrastructure; algorithmic visibility as a market-access mechanism; intelligent finance as a resource-orchestration capability; platform opacity and dependency; ecosystem asymmetry between Pakistan and China; and human judgment, trust, and governance as boundary conditions. The comparative findings indicate that AI-enabled financial intelligence creates value most effectively when entrepreneurs can convert data into action and when digital platforms provide stable routes to visibility, legitimacy, and customers. The study contributes an ecosystem-level explanation linking entrepreneurial alertness, dynamic capabilities, digital finance, and algorithmic visibility.
