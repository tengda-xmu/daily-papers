研究问题

论文针对统一类药性标准与靶标家族需求不匹配的问题。摘要指出，不同家族配体占据不同理化空间，单一标准可能“reward some target classes while penalising others”。这涉及筛选及生成奖励中的评价偏差，而非直接预测药效。[摘要]

方法与路线

FamDLike采用靶标家族特异的类药性建模，结合期望度函数、广义加性模型与树模型。摘要明确三部分“can also be used independently”，可按可解释性或预测灵活性的优先级选择；但未说明输入描述符、训练标签、模型参数和数据划分。[摘要]

创新与比较

解读：主要方法特色是把全局统一评价转向靶标家族条件下的评价，并通过三种互补模型覆盖可解释性与灵活性的不同需求。作者将其定位为现有评分的“target-aware complement”，而非全面替代；摘要不足以核实其相对既有工作的首创性。[摘要]

查看公开摘要

Drug-likeness is a central metric which is used to evaluate the physicochemical profile of small molecules either as a filter for virtual screening or, more recently, as a reward function in generative design tasks. Established scores such as QED are widely adopted for these purposes, although the ligands of different therapeutic target families occupy different regions of physicochemical space. Applying a single global drug-likeness criterion can therefore systematically reward some target classes while penalising others, narrowing the chemical space considered druglike in a way that may not reflect the actual requirements for a given project. In this work, we propose a target family specific drug-likeness approach, the FamDLike framework, designed to distinguish target families more accurately than existing metrics and to explore family specific druglike regions of chemical space. This framework combines three complementary modelling paradigms: desirability functions, generalized additive models and tree-based machine learning models that span the interpretability-flexibility spectrum. The three components can also be used independently, depending on whether interpretability or predictive flexibility is prioritized. The abilities of the proposed method have been tested on real-world use cases in computational medicinal chemistry: an independent set from DrugBank, library screening of Enamine and KLIFS compounds, and de novo molecule generation via REINVENT4. The proposed approach therefore provides a more discriminative, interpretable and target-aware complement to existing drug-likeness scores, suitable for ranking in early discovery pipelines and as a reward function for generative chemistry. Furthermore, a user-friendly web portal (https://huggingface.co/spaces/kochgroup/FamDLike) is available to obtain drug-likeness profiles of molecules.
